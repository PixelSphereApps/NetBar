## Everything at a Glance

Net Bar provides deep insights into your system performance without cluttering your workspace.

### Core Monitoring
- **System Health:** Monitor CPU load, Memory pressure, and Disk storage in a beautifully unified dashboard.
- **Real-time Network:** Instantaneous download and upload speeds with detailed Wi-Fi diagnostics and latency tracking.
- **Energy & Thermals:** Stay informed about your battery health and device temperature to maintain peak performance.

### Network Tools
- **VPN Status:** Instantly see your VPN connection status and quickly identify active VPN configurations.
- **Public IP:** Track your public IP address and location information with automatic updates and copy functionality.
- **DNS Quick Switcher:** Switch between popular DNS providers (Google, Cloudflare, Quad9) with one click for better privacy and speed.

### Advanced Features
- **Total Customization:** Drag-and-drop reordering, dynamic filtering, and fine-tuned typography control.
- **Unstack Speeds:** Optionally separate upload and download displays for a cleaner menu bar layout.
- **WiFi Channel Scanner:** Analyze nearby networks, identify channel congestion, and optimize your WiFi for the best performance.

### Smart Alerts
- **Smart Tips:** Get context-aware alerts based on hardware status like high memory usage or weak signals.
- **Network Sound Alerts:** Get instantly notified with a sound when your Wi-Fi drops, internet goes down, ping spikes, or packet loss crosses your custom threshold.
- **Native Notifications:** Receive rich, integrated macOS notifications for critical network drops, high background data usage, and status alerts.

### Additional Tools
- **Captive Portal Detection:** Automatically detect and notify you of captive portals on public Wi-Fi networks for seamless authentication.
- **Scheduled Speed Tests:** Set up automated periodic speed testing and build a history of your true network performance over time.
- **Bandwidth Limits:** Track your data usage with precise daily and monthly limits, and get notified natively before you hit the cap.
